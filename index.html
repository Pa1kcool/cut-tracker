Swaps & rules

Stay Flexible

  • Chicken ⇄ eggs / dal / paneer
  • Rice ⇄ oats / extra bread
  • Curd ⇄ milk + whey

3–4L water daily · weigh food week 1 · 1 bigger meal/week is fine. Hit protein every day.
